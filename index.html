In [04]: manifesto.read()

  
“We keep the competitive spirit, and we evolve what we compete on.”


  

    
01 · our fire is back
Through Kaggle- and Zindi-style hackathons, the current leadership turned a quiet club into an active, competitive community. I want to honour that work and build on it.

    
02 · AI is moving
Today's AI engineers build agents, knowledge assistants, evaluations and products that work in the real world, not just models that score well on a leaderboard. Our community should prepare us for that world.

    
03 · my vision
Keep what works, grow what's next, and turn CMU-Africa Data Science from a club into a community: a place where we can research anything, build anything, and connect with the world's leading AI companies.

    In [06]: agenda[CGI]
    
    
    My agenda
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    

    
    
      
    01
    Rooted in Community DNA
    
        
    Everything we do builds on the spirit that brought this club back to life. Our community stays open, welcoming and member-led, for beginners and experts alike.
    
        
    What we keep: the energy, teamwork and competitive spirit that revived the club.
    
        
    How we move forward: regular member feedback, open planning of activities, and peer-to-peer learning and mentorship at the heart of everything we do.
    
      
    02
    Compete to Build
    
        
    
        
    What we keep: the Kaggle- and Zindi-style competitions, and entering global challenges as a club.
    
        
    How we move forward: new build tracks in AI agents, RAG and knowledge assistants, benchmarks and evaluations, AI reasoning and AI safety, judged on live demos, real-world usefulness and technical depth. Beginner-friendly learning tracks so every member can take part.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    03
    OpenLab: Research Anything, Build Anything
    
        
    A space where any member can turn an idea into a project or a research paper, with the support to see it through.
    
        
    • Project teams and research groups working on real problems
    • Collaboration with the CMU-Africa Research Club and faculty for mentorship
    • Support to submit and present work at conferences such as Deep Learning Indaba
    • Regular showcases where members demo what they've built
    
    
    
    
    
      
    04
    Pan-African Collaboration
    
        
    Our members come from across Africa, and most of us will build our careers here. Our community should be connected to the whole continent.
    
        
    
        
    • Partnerships with university and machine learning communities
    • Joint hackathons, events and speaker exchanges
    • Fellowships, conferences and programmes beyond our campus
    
      
    05
    Industry and Global Partnerships
    
        
    Bringing the AI industry closer to members through Kaggle, Zindi, NVIDIA, and AI companies across Africa and beyond.
    
        
    
        
    • Opportunities: internships, jobs and competitions
    • Knowledge: workshops, training and certifications
    • Technology: compute, tools and datasets
    • Careers: portfolio reviews and direct connections to employers
    
      
    06
    CMU-Africa AI Engineering Summit
    
        
    The first annual summit, launching Fall 2027. Details below.
    
        
    • Talks, workshops, demo day and hackathon finals
    • Talent showcase and investor session

    CMU-Africa AI Engineering Summit
    
        
    Africa has the talent to be the core engineers behind the world's leading AI systems. The world just needs to see it. In Fall 2027 we'll host the first annual summit and engage frontier AI companies and labs such as NVIDIA, Google DeepMind and Anthropic, along with global tech companies and investors, to meet the engineers building AI in Africa.
    
        
    
        
    Having helped organise AI Summer of Code's Synopsis Conference and supported DataFest Africa and PyCon Africa, I know what it takes to deliver a conference of this scale.
